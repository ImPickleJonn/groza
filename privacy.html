GROZA — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-05-21

GROZA is a free-to-play game distributed as a Telegram Mini App. We respect your privacy and collect as little data as possible.

What we collect

  • Telegram identifiers — your Telegram user ID and (if set) username, only when you complete a purchase using Telegram Stars, so we can verify and credit the purchase to your account.
  • Engagement events — when you open the game, we log an "open" event tied to your Telegram user ID for product analytics. No game-state telemetry beyond opens.
  • Game progress — coins, unlocks, settings, and achievements are stored locally in your browser via localStorage. This data never leaves your device unless you make a Stars purchase.

What we do NOT collect

  • No real name, email, phone number, or contacts
  • No location data
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No data shared with third-party advertisers — there are no ads in GROZA

Telegram Stars

Stars purchases are processed by Telegram. We receive the transaction confirmation (Stars amount + your user ID) so we can grant the in-game item. We do not see your payment method.

Data retention

Purchase records are kept for accounting purposes for the period required by applicable law. Engagement events are retained for product analytics for up to 12 months and then deleted or anonymized.

Your rights

To request deletion of your data, contact us via the bot's owner DM or the channel linked from @GrozaGameBot.

Changes

If we update this policy, we'll bump the "Last updated" date at the top.
